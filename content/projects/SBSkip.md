SB Skip is an Android app that automatically removes SponsorBlock segments from downloaded YouTube videos, helping students save time by skipping sponsored sections and other unwanted segments.

The app can download videos or playlists using NewPipe, automatically detect downloaded media files, retrieve SponsorBlock segment data, and process the videos to skip or remove those segments. Students can run the project by cloning the repository and building the Android app with Gradle. Contributions are welcome, especially improvements to media detection, SponsorBlock integration, video processing, and the Android UI.
